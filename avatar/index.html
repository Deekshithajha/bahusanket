What this shows

  • Retargeting: each frame, the points are turned into joint angles for the avatar's skeleton (shoulders, elbows, wrists, every finger joint) and into face settings (blink, mouth shape, head turn).
  • Depth matters: turning a wrist or bending fingers toward the camera needs the z (depth) value. Our training data keeps only x and y today; the raw files on Drive still have z.
  • Face: mouth shapes use the upper and lower inner lip, and blinks use the eyelids. Those are the points the 123-point layout added.
