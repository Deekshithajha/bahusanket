What this shows

  • Retargeting: each frame, the points are turned into joint angles for the avatar's skeleton (shoulders, elbows, wrists, every finger joint) and into face settings (blink, mouth shape, head turn).
  • Model output: text model with guidance s = 4, length control so each clip lasts about as long as real signing of the sentence, extra detail codes drawn by the predictor, then the bone-length fix (notebooks 11 to 16).
  • Depth: our text model writes only x and y. A separate depth model (notebook 18) reads that movement and predicts how far forward every point is, so wrists turn and fingers bend toward you. On real test clips its palm direction is off by about 7°, and it follows real wrist depth far better than the old guess did. Switch Depth to compare it with MediaPipe's own depth (real signing only) or the old guess from arm-bone length. The three “with depth” clips at the bottom of the list use MediaPipe depth.
  • Face: mouth shapes use the upper and lower inner lip, and blinks use the eyelids. Those are the points the 123-point layout added.
